# IntegrationIQ

IntegrationIQ helps product and technology partnership teams answer a question before involving engineering: **Can our product integrate with this tool, and what do we still need to find out?**

## Why it exists

Assessing a potential integration often means searching developer portals, reading technical documentation, and trying to separate what is possible from what requires partner approval or customer permission. IntegrationIQ brings that research into a concise, evidence-backed brief that Product Managers can understand.

## What it does

Enter a product and describe the integration you want to offer your customers. IntegrationIQ researches official vendor documentation and presents:

- Whether the proposed integration appears feasible
- Which requested capabilities are confirmed, unverified, or unavailable
- Access requirements and potential blockers
- Questions to ask the vendor before moving forward
- Supporting source links and expandable technical evidence

## Current status

A working V1 prototype. It prioritizes clear uncertainty and official documentation over confident guesses. Findings are documentation-based assessments, not tested integrations, and should be verified with the vendor before committing to a partnership.

IntegrationIQ does not generate integration code or estimate engineering effort. API credentials are configured on the server; end users do not supply keys.
